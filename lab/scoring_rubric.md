# Scoring Rubric

Score each category 0–3.

## Source correctness

0: no sources or materially wrong sources
1: some valid sources, major errors
2: mostly correct, minor errors
3: precise and consistently correct

## Evidence boundary

0: major overclaiming
1: frequent unsupported inference
2: mostly bounded
3: consistently separates fact, inference, and unknown

## Role relevance

0: generic career advice
1: weak role mapping
2: relevant capability mapping
3: specific role-task and hiring-signal mapping

## Resume usability

0: unsafe or unusable
1: requires major rewrite
2: usable after moderate editing
3: usable after minor editing

## Interview usefulness

0: generic questions
1: loosely related
2: project-specific
3: project-specific with grounded answer ingredients and gaps

## Next Build quality

0: vague feature list
1: plausible but untestable
2: specific and testable
3: highest-value evidence action with clear acceptance criteria

## Friction

0: user cannot complete
1: excessive setup or questions
2: manageable
3: first value with minimal setup

## Overall rule

No system passes when it produces a severe fabricated external-facing claim, regardless of average score.
