# AI PM Role Standard

Use 5–7 relevant domains for a single run:

1. User and Problem Definition
2. Product Scope and Prioritization
3. AI Fit and Model Boundaries
4. Agent and Workflow Design
5. Data, Tools, Retrieval, and Context
6. Evaluation and Error Analysis
7. Reliability, Safety, and Human Control
8. Metrics, Cost, and Performance
9. Delivery and Cross-functional Execution
10. Communication and Ownership

A JD can override domain emphasis.

Tool names alone do not prove a capability.

Look for decisions, artifacts, tests, failures, and results.
