# Project2Job Day 5 Career-Asset Dogfood

Recorded: 2026-07-28

Evidence class: product-owner target-user dogfood. The participant is both the
product owner and a member of the intended early-career AI PM target-user
profile. This is not independent user validation.

## Observed session

- Asset: a Project2Job Day 5 interview answer.
- Rating: 8/10.
- Stated use: the participant would use the asset after
  minor-to-moderate editing. Actual external use was not observed.
- Bad case: evidence-safety warnings leaked into the copyable interview script.
  Pending validation, failed gates, and missing outcomes became more memorable
  than the supported achievement, product judgment, and role relevance.
- Correction: the final ad hoc revision separated a strong external interview
  script from private warnings. The correction was accepted as the desired
  behavior.

## Product implication

The session supports a product-rule change: external career assets should use
the strongest defensible interpretation of supported facts, while nonmaterial
risks remain available in private preparation structures. It does not weaken
the historical-fact boundary or permit invented ownership, users, metrics,
production status, outcomes, or stakeholder relationships.

The observed Day 5 failure story is retained as a behavioral regression in
`skill/p2j/evals/behavior_cases.jsonl`. The case tests the ending and disclosure
properties rather than one exact script.

## Measurement boundary

The session does not establish:

- independent user preference or validation;
- actual application or interview use;
- hiring impact;
- blind-review preference over the baseline;
- completion of Day 5.

First-use timing and a complete structured pilot record were not captured.
Blind human review and disagreement adjudication remain separate release
requirements.
